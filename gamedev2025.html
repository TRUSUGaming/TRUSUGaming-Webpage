June 23–27, 2025 · Thompson Rivers University, Kamloops

Game Dev Summer School 2025

Our third summer school was a week of practical game design. Every morning, attendees worked through hands-on design sessions that built up to a playable prototype by Friday. Afternoons brought crash courses, industry talks and round tables.
